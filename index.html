Denzix Bank App

Developed an AI-powered digital banking application that modernizes banking through intelligent automation and secure financial services. The platform enables users to manage accounts, transfer funds, pay bills, and track transactions seamlessly.

AI features provide personalized financial insights, spending analysis, fraud detection, and virtual customer support. The solution enhances customer experience while improving operational efficiency and supporting smarter financial decision-making.

Developed a modern online Nigerian restaurant platform that showcases a wide variety of freshly prepared local delicacies. The application allows customers to browse menus, explore meal categories, place orders, and enjoy a seamless ordering experience.

Built with a responsive, user-friendly interface, it promotes authentic homemade cuisine while making food ordering fast, convenient, and accessible across devices.

Developed a responsive alumni portal for the Abia State Polytechnic Accountancy Class of 2010 to strengthen communication and lifelong connections among graduates.
